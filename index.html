01 — About

Field notes on a life in geography

Eonsu — officially known as Ansula Basumatary — is a postgraduate student pursuing Post Graduate Degree in Geography under the School of Sustainable Tribal Resource Management (STRM) at the Kalinga Institute of Social Sciences (KISS), Deemed to be University, Bhubaneswar.

A 2024 graduate of U N Academy, Kokrajhar (affiliated with Bodoland University, Kokrajhar, Assam), Eonsu's undergraduate dissertation traced long-term land surface temperature trends in Bongaigaon using multi-temporal satellite data — work that continues to shape a research interest in how satellite-derived data can inform climate-aware, equitable urban futures.

Alongside research, Eonsu serves as a Gender Champion at KISS-DU, helping lead campus conversations on gender equity, and recently spoke as a panelist at the 11th International Conference on Life Skill Education (ICLSE 2026), on a session exploring how life skill education can disrupt patriarchy and build equitable futures.

"Geography is never only physical — every contour line, heat signature, and land-use change eventually traces back to people, and to power."

05 — Project

Featured research

Undergraduate Dissertation · 2024

Long-Term Trends in Land Surface Temperature of Bongaigaon Using Multi-Temporal Satellite Data

U N Academy, Kokrajhar — Affiliated with Bodoland University, Assam

This dissertation investigated decadal-scale changes in Land Surface Temperature (LST) across Bongaigaon using multi-temporal satellite imagery and GIS-based retrieval techniques. By comparing thermal signatures across different time periods, the study explored the relationship between land-use change, urban growth, and rising surface temperatures — contributing to a clearer picture of how remote sensing can support climate monitoring and urban planning decisions in growing Assamese towns.
